## 1️⃣ Overview  

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is rapidly reshaping the entire healthcare ecosystem—from clinical decision‑support and diagnostics to drug discovery, patient monitoring, and health‑system operations. The convergence of massive data, advanced algorithms (especially deep learning), and ever‑greater compute power has accelerated AI adoption across hospitals, biotech firms, and health‑tech startups.

## 7️⃣ Challenges & Open Research Questions  

1. **Data Quality & Bias** – Scarcity of diverse, high‑quality annotated datasets can propagate health disparities.  
2. **Interpretability** – Clinicians require explainable AI (XAI); methods such as SHAP, attention maps, and counterfactuals are under active development.  
3. **Clinical Integration** – Seamless EMR integration, workflow alignment, and low inference latency remain technical hurdles.  
4. **Ethical & Legal Liability** – Attribution of responsibility for AI‑driven errors is still unresolved.  
5. **Sustainability** – Large models consume considerable energy; research into “green AI” (model pruning, low‑power inference) is emerging.
